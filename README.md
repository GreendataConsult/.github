Greendata is an environmental consultancy that turns data into impact. Working hand in hand with local partners between Bogotá and Bordeaux, we help companies, funds and institutions measure their environmental footprint, understand where they can act and finance the projects that make a real difference.

### What we do

🌱 **Green Tech.** We build digital tools that measure carbon footprints, identify reduction pathways and support the design of high quality offsetting strategies.

💶 **Green Finance.** We work with funds, banks and multilateral institutions to strengthen impact measurement and build sustainable finance systems.

🌳 **Project Development.** From feasibility assessments through implementation and monitoring, we deliver nature based projects that create measurable environmental and social value.

### Our values

**Integrity**, because a footprint is only useful when its methodology can be trusted.
**Innovation**, so that rigorous climate science becomes practical, everyday tools.
**Impact**, since every analysis we deliver should lead to concrete action.
